RedCrow — Insector Tweaks v0.6 XML

SKILL SPECIALIZATION GROUPS

Combat:
- Shooting: +5 aptitude, passion +1 level, reload time -50%, aiming time -50%,
  shooting accuracy +0.5.
- Melee: +5 aptitude, passion +1 level, melee cooldown x0.5,
  melee dodge chance x1.5.
- Only one Combat specialization.
- Combat blocks all Craft and Intellectual specializations and their work.

Craft:
- Cooking: CookSpeed x1.25, ButcheryFleshSpeed x1.25, food poisoning x0.
- Mining: MiningSpeed x1.25, DeepDrillingSpeed x1.25.
- Animals: AnimalGatherSpeed x1.5, TameAnimalChance x1.5,
  TrainAnimalChance x1.5.
- Plants: PlantWorkSpeed x1.25, PlantHarvestYield x1.25.
- Crafting: Smelting, mechanoid butchery, smithing, stonecutting,
  electronics and tailoring speed x1.25.
- Construction: ConstructSuccessChance x2, ConstructionSpeed x1.25,
  SmoothingSpeed x1.5.
- Only one Craft specialization.
- Craft blocks all Combat and Intellectual specializations and their work.

Intellectual:
- Artistic: +5 aptitude and passion +1 level.
- Social: negotiation, trade-price improvement, social impact,
  arrest success and conversion power x1.5; pawn beauty +3.
- Medicine: operation speed x1.25, surgery success x2,
  tend speed and quality x1.25.
- Intellectual: ResearchSpeed x1.25.
- Any number of Intellectual specializations.
- Intellectual blocks all Combat and Craft specializations and their work.

IMPORTANT:
- Reload time -50% is implemented as ReloadSpeed x2.
- Animal work has no generic speed stat. The specialization improves
  resource gathering speed plus taming and training success chances.
- Quality bonuses require a DLL and are listed in QUALITY_BONUSES_REQUIRE_DLL.txt.

All previous v0.5 balance and compatibility changes are preserved.
