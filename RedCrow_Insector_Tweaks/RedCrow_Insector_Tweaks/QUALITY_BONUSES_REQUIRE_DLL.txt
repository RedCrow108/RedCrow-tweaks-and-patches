RED CROW — QUALITY BONUS LIMITATIONS

This build implements every requested effect that GeneDef XML can express.

Not implemented in this XML-only build:
- Crafting: +1 product quality.
- Construction: +1 construction quality.
- Artistic: +7 product quality / guaranteed legendary products.

RimWorld determines created-item and construction quality in game code rather
than through an ordinary pawn StatDef that GeneDef XML can offset. These three
effects require a small compiled C# / Harmony assembly.

Everything else in the requested Combat, Craft and Intellectual
specializations is included.
